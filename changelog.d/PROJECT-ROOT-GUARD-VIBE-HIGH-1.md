### Development

- Guard project-root discovery against unrelated nested Git repositories: `find_root` remembers the first Git boundary (a `.git` entry — full, unborn or linked-worktree) on the way up and accepts the enclosing workspace only when that boundary is its main clone (any name, a direct child of the root) or a registered worker worktree (`.unio-worker` marker, or a direct child of `wt/` for pre-marker workspaces). Setters, stop/resume and every other root-acting command now refuse before any policy write, lock or provider call when launched from an unrelated nested repository, explaining the boundary and pointing at local `unio init` or the intended project; benign-fallback callers that treat a missing root as "use the explicit `--project`" stay quiet. Offline regressions in `tests/unio-project-root.py` freeze the outer policy/STOP bytes and fail on the previous code.
