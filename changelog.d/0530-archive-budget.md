- Development addition for the 0.5.30 archive-budget contract (docs/development/contracts/0530-ARCHIVE-BUDGET.md): the explicit `unio save archive-worker --apply` operation honors optional `UNIO_SAVE_ARCHIVE_TIMEOUT` (positive whole seconds 1–900, default 60 when unset) as one shared monotonic deadline across its inventory recheck, selected deep validation, atomic moves and post-move rechecks, with no inner fixed bound. Invalid values refuse planning and application before locks, receipts or any store effect. The read-only plan keeps its fixed 30-second budget and reports the configured apply budget additively; intent, outcome and partial receipts plus plan/apply outputs gain an additive `apply_budget_seconds` field. Capture, inspect, restore and continuation budgets, caps, pins, deep integrity, locks and all existing refusal outcomes are unchanged, and nothing archives, purges or retries automatically. Offline tests in `tests/unio-save-archive-worker.py` prove default, configured and enforced deadlines through a fake elapsed clock without real 60-second waits.
